Notes from my desk

Most of my projects begin with a practical question: can a student process be less frustrating, can an online conversation leave fewer traces, or can information be easier to find?

I learn by building the whole path—from interface and server logic to database and deployment. Game development is the next path I’m working toward because it brings together systems, drawing, interaction, and storytelling.

How I work

  • Start with the problem. I want to know who is stuck and why before choosing a framework.
  • Make it work, then make it clear. I build a complete path, test it, and simplify what feels awkward.
  • Follow the unfamiliar parts. Real-time events, APIs, and mobile development became skills because a project needed them.
  • Stay open to another perspective. Useful feedback is part of the build, not an interruption.
  • Use AI with ownership. I use it to explore and debug faster, then review, understand, and take responsibility for what ships.
